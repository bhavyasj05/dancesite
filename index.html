Our Story

Established 2015 · Bangalore, Karnataka
Offline Classes
Online Classes

Gejjenadha Nruthya Mandir (R) was founded in 2015 by Smt. Bhuvana H S Gowda with a singular vision — to make the classical art of Bharatanatyam accessible to every child and adult across Karnataka, without compromising on the rigour and devotion that this sacred tradition demands.

What began as a single studio has grown, over ten-plus years, into a network of more than 30 branches spanning the length and breadth of Karnataka. Today, students learn both in our physical studios and through our structured online programme — bringing the sound of gejje (ankle bells) into homes across the state and beyond.

Our performances have graced international stages in Malaysia and Mount Abu, and we have performed across sacred and cultural destinations in India — Tirupati, Mantralaya, Shirdi, and many more. In total, Gejjenadha Nruthya Mandir has presented over 2,000 programs.
